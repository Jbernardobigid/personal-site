# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

Tech professionals, potential employers, collaborators, and community members who discover Jorge online — across data security and AI governance circles, Afro-Brazilian cultural spaces, and cycling communities. Audience spans three languages (PT, EN, ES) and four continents. They arrive with different entry points (LinkedIn, cycling community, referral) and need to leave understanding the same person.

## Product Purpose

Personal portfolio for Jorge Bernardo — Sr. Technical Trainer at BigID, founder of DePretoPraPreto (Afro-Brazilian cycling brand) and afterALL. The site exists to establish a unified identity across worlds that don't usually intersect: data security / AI governance expertise, cultural entrepreneurship, and high-performance cycling.

The intersection is the product. Jorge is not a tech person who cycles on weekends. He is not a cultural founder who also works in enterprise software. Both are equally true, equally central. The site succeeds when a visitor leaves thinking "I didn't know one person could hold all of this" — and wants to reach out.

## Typography (Official)

From the brand guide. The site correctly implements both. Do not substitute.

**Primary — Noto Serif Display Extra Condensed**
Chosen for "presença vertical, imponente e refinada." Evoking "sofisticação com tensão, como quem anda ereto mesmo quando o mundo tenta dobrar." Near-liturgical and editorial simultaneously. Transmits "autoridade silenciosa, ideal para alguém que não se explica, se posiciona."
Use for: titles, name, institutional pieces, video covers, leadership and manifesto elements.

**Secondary — Lora**
Chosen for "natureza equilibrada e sensível." "Tipografia de leitura fluida, intelectual, mas com curvas orgânicas que evocam humanidade." Represents the analytical, narrative, and welcoming side of Jorge's brand — the mentor, the strategist.
Use for: running text, captions, descriptions, emails, narrative content.

The brand guide summary: "Enquanto a Noto Serif traz o peso do protagonismo, da autoridade e da presença ancestral, Lora atua como contraponto emocional e intelectual."

## DePretoPraPreto vs. Personal Brand: Two Distinct Visual Identities

**DePretoPraPreto** has its own visual identity: hot pink/magenta, electric blue, bold yellow/gold, Adinkra symbols, heavy typography, communal and celebratory. Loud by design — Black liberation energy made visible.

**Jorge Bernardo (personal brand)** is the opposite register: warm dark luxury, editorial restraint, terracotta warmth, neo-noir precision. The contrast is load-bearing. Never let the personal site borrow DPPP's energy — they each gain authority by being different. The Adinkra symbol in DPPP belongs to that brand; do not import it decoratively into the personal site.

## Anti-references

- **Generic SaaS portfolio** — the Notion/Linear/startup-founder template: navy + white, feature list, CTA grid, sanitized "professional" aesthetic. Has no soul.
- **LinkedIn-style resume** — timeline-heavy, credential-first, corporate. Looks like a formatted PDF. Signals conformity, not vision.
- **Hypebeast / streetwear overload** — all uppercase, neon, brutal type. Treats aesthetics as identity without depth or earned authority.
- **Generic Black creative portfolio** — Afrocentric motifs used decoratively (kente patterns, generic Pan-African palette) without substance. Cultural presence must come from point of view, not decoration.

## Design Principles

1. **The intersection is the story.** Never let the site feel like two separate identities stapled together. Tech precision and cultural depth speak the same design language — warm, rigorous, and impossible to reduce to a single category.

2. **Earned luxury.** Warmth and restraint over flash. The terracotta is character, not decoration — it comes from real life, from his wardrobe in Madrid. Grain, shadow, and restraint signal taste more than gradient effects ever will.

3. **Show over tell.** A full-bleed photograph of Jorge on the bike says more than a credential list. The cycling mosaic, the brand photography, the hero image — these carry the argument. Copy reinforces; it doesn't lead. The photo library is large — use it.

4. **Cultural presence, not cultural decoration.** Afrofuturismo as worldview, not aesthetic motif. Nothing signals merely. Every design choice has a reason; nothing is there to "feel Black" without meaning it. The DPPP Adinkra symbol belongs to that brand — don't import it into the personal site as decoration.

5. **Precision at every scale.** From type metrics to hover states to scroll behavior, everything is intentional or it doesn't exist. A sloppy detail anywhere undermines the authority the design is trying to claim.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

No formal WCAG target specified. Ship the best experience for the primary audience. Address accessibility issues when they surface — particularly contrast for the terracotta-on-dark palette, and keyboard navigation for the custom cursor interactions.
